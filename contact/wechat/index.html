WeChat Is Not a Contact Option

Why I no longer use WeChat, and how to reach me instead

Notice: WeChat is no longer provided as a public contact method due to significant security and privacy concerns, documented below.

Summary

After multiple documented incidents, I consider WeChat an unsuitable platform for communication. This page lists the specific issues that led to this decision.

Documented Incidents

Data exposure

Personal data exposed via Telegram social-engineering databases

  • WeChat user data frequently appears in “social engineering databases” (社工庫) on Telegram.
  • Attackers can dox people (開盒) using a WeChat ID to obtain real names and ID numbers, home addresses, phone numbers, and associated accounts.
Data breach

Shanghai Suishenma (隨申碼) health-code leak

  • A massive data breach exposed millions of Shanghai residents.
  • WeChat integration allowed health data to be correlated with personal identities.
  • It demonstrated a systemic weakness in WeChat-integrated government systems.
Privacy

Unauthorized hardware access on Android

  • Documented cases of WeChat accessing the camera without user consent.
  • Microphone activation in background processes.
  • Continuous location tracking even when location services are disabled.
  • Network traffic analysis shows data sent to undisclosed servers.
Censorship

Content manipulation and surveillance

  • Automatic deletion of WeChat Official Account articles (公眾號文章).
  • Real-time monitoring of Moments (朋友圈) with retroactive content removal.
  • Account suspension without an appeal process.
  • Keyword filtering that affects private conversations.
  • Image recognition for “sensitive” content.

Security research has also revealed:

  • Mini-programs lacking proper sandboxing
  • Voice message analysis and transcription without consent
  • Cross-app data sharing with other Tencent products

If You Still Use WeChat

  1. Disable location services completely.
  2. Revoke camera and microphone permissions.
  3. Use a separate phone number for everything else.
  4. Never share sensitive information via WeChat.
  5. Regularly audit and delete your chat history.
  6. Consider migrating to another platform entirely.
