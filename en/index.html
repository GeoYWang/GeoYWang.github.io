🔬 Research

Kinematic Analysis of Elytra Flight as a Discrete Dynamical System: Energy Injection and Perpetual Motion in Minecraft Physics

PreprintApplied MathPython

Modeled Minecraft's Elytra flight as a 20 Hz discrete dynamical system and derived closed-form fixed points for both gliding and climbing. Proved analytically that θ = 0° is the globally optimal pitch, with an exact asymptotic glide ratio of 495/49 ≈ 10.102 : 1, and showed global linear convergence (ρ = 0.99) via the Jacobian's eigenvalues. Asymmetric momentum-transfer coefficients reveal a net energy-injection mechanism worth +3.66 blocks of height per cycle.
